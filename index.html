What We Do

Full-service remodeling, renovation and restoration.

Most Kansas City contractors specialize in one or two trades. Build Wiser covers the whole scope, with crews trained for occupied multi-family work and a dedicated supervisor on every project.

Multi-Family Remodeling

Unit turns, interior renovations, and capital improvement projects scheduled around occupancy so residents stay put and units come back online fast.

Water & Flood Damage

Extraction, drying, and full rebuild after burst pipes, roof leaks, and supply line failures. Fast response keeps a small loss from becoming a large one.

Fire & Smoke Restoration

Board-up, soot and odor removal, structural repair, and reconstruction — coordinated with adjusters from the first walkthrough through final punch.

Mold Remediation

Containment, removal, and remediation to industry standards, with the moisture source corrected so the problem does not come back next season.

Storm & Roofing

Wind, hail, and storm response including roofing, siding, gutters, and concrete — documented for the claim as the work proceeds.

General Contracting

Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, drywall, and concrete under one contract — one point of contact instead of five subs and five schedules.
